# Demand Interviews (PRD §15.2)

Not user acquisition and not evidence of learning gains. Record facts only; report as "M of N said …".

Questions (after a 15-minute screen-share demo):
1. How do you (or your students) currently do research projects?
2. Where do you get stuck most?
3. In what situation would you use this?
4. Who would pay for this — student, parent, school, nobody?
5. Which part is least necessary?
